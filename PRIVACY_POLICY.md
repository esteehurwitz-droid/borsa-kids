# Privacy Policy - בורסת הילדים (Kids Stock Exchange)

**Effective Date:** 2026-07-06

## 1. Overview

בורסת הילדים ("the App") is an **educational stock market simulation game**. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 2. Data We Collect

### What We DO Collect:
- **Game Progress:** Your portfolio, trades, XP, and leaderboard position (stored locally on your device)
- **Language Preference:** Your chosen language (Hebrew, English, Arabic, Russian)
- **Username & Avatar:** Display name only (not linked to identity)

### What We DO NOT Collect:
- ❌ No personal information (name, email, phone, address)
- ❌ No location data
- ❌ No device identifiers or cookies
- ❌ No financial information or real payment data
- ❌ No advertising data or tracking pixels

## 3. How We Use Data

All game data is stored **locally on your device**. We do NOT:
- Send data to servers
- Share data with third parties
- Use data for advertising or profiling
- Sell or monetize user data

## 4. Live Market Data

The App displays **real stock prices and market data** from:
- **Finnhub API** (https://finnhub.io)
- **exchangerate-api.com** (currency conversion)

These services have their own privacy policies. The App does not send your personal data to these services—only market data requests.

## 5. Children's Privacy

This App is designed for **ages 10+**. We comply with:
- **COPPA** (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) - US
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) - EU
- **LGPD** (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados) - Brazil

**We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.**

## 6. Data Security

- All game data stored locally on your device
- No data transmission to external servers
- Uses industry-standard HTTPS for API calls

## 7. Your Rights

You can:
- Clear all game data anytime (delete from device)
- Switch languages without data loss
- Download and backup your progress (export feature, coming soon)

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any updates.
